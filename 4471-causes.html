Shared parser

The shared parser reads every date month first. For tenants whose locale writes the day first, the month and day are swapped. This affects app and email reports, plus form reports that do not come through booking-v3's month-first hint.

Booking-v3 hint

Booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY. People follow that hint, but day-first tenants interpret the submitted value in their locale's order. These form reports need a separate fix from the shared parser.
